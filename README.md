# JunkManagementSolution

It can be difficult and time-consuming for household and commercial users to manage garbage disposal. They frequently have trouble locating reputable junk management businesses, getting in touch with them, and keeping tabs on their disposal operations. On the other hand, garbage management businesses struggle to handle requests from clients, control correspondence, and keep track of past client services. Our website eliminates the need for back-and-forth communication over numerous channels, simplifying the rubbish disposal procedure. Also, our website provides access to prior actions, quotations, and correspondence, facilitating mutual organization.

Some of the key goals of this website are as follows:

User Registration and Login: Easy onboarding procedure that lets users register and log in. 
Nearby Junk providers: Depending on where they are, users can peruse a list of junk management providers. 
Upload Image: For a more precise quote request, users can upload pictures of the trash they wish to get rid of.
History Tracking: Users can examine historical requests, quotes, and provided services.
Service management: makes it simple to keep track of pending requests and rendered services.

The goal of this web-based platform is to make waste management organizations and end users' lives easier by streamlining the junk disposal procedure. It's simple for users to sign up, log in, and peruse nearby junk management providers. They will also be able to see their service history and previous activity.
